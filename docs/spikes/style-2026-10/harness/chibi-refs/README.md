# Chibi hair references (2026-10-24)

User: « je ne veux pas une copie-conforme de cette chevelure ! tu as enquêté sur des exemples
chibi de chevelure au moins ? ». The Meshy « Midnight Ponytail » sets the **level of finish**,
not the style; the style bar must be chibi. Until the user picks one, `hair-judge` must not be
run against the Meshy capture alone (it pulls towards realistic strands).

Source: Sketchfab public search API (`api.sketchfab.com/v3/search`, queries chibi hair, chibi
unicorn, chibi pony, nendoroid hair, kawaii / stylized / cute unicorn, toon hair; ~250 results
looked at as contact sheets), then the largest thumbnail (1920×1080) of the shortlist, saved
here. References only: no asset is downloaded or reused.

What they share (looked at, not measured): **sculpted locks, not strands** — few big masses
broken into medium locks with crisp edges and pointed tips, layering (bangs over side locks
over back mass), one or two colour streaks, hair hugging the head. Finesse comes from the
sharpness of edges and tips and the layering, not from strand count. This is why the Meshy
strand groom cannot be the style target.
